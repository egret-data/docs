# Overview

**Governance → Overview** shows what needs attention and what changed recently.

![Governance overview](/img/platform/governance-overview.png)

The header counts **catalogs**, **egret grants**, **egret rules** and **classifications**. **Needs attention** lists problems worth fixing, and **Recent changes** lists governance changes as they are recorded. Recording began when this version was installed, so earlier changes are not listed.

The overview does not check views that may expose classified data through `DEFINER`, or classification coverage; see [Classifications](./classifications).

The Governance sidebar groups its pages into **Data**, **People & groups**, **Classification**, **Lineage** and **Engine & policy**.
