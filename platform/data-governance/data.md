# Data

**Governance → Data** is where you look at one dataset's access: who can read it, its tags and its rules.

![Governance data](/img/platform/governance-data.png)

The left tree lists the **catalogs** the platform knows about (for example an Iceberg catalog). Expand a catalog to its schemas and tables, filter the loaded items, or type a path such as `catalog.schema.table` in **Go to** to open a table directly. Opening a catalog, schema or table shows who can read it, its tags and its rules.

An administrator adds catalogs under [Control Plane → Catalogs](../control-plane/catalogs).
