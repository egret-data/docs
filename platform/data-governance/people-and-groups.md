# People & groups

**Governance → People & groups** shows users, groups and roles, and the egret grants and rules that reach each one.

![People and groups](/img/platform/governance-people.png)

Four tabs: **Users**, **Groups**, **Roles** and **Unmatched names**. The Users tab lists people with their email and username, a search box and a **Show service accounts** switch. People appear here once they have signed in through SSO, together with the groups the identity provider reports.

The shield button (top right) checks a person's access.
