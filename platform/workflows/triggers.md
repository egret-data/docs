# Triggers

A trigger starts a flow on a schedule or an event. It is declared under `triggers:` in the flow's YAML and then appears here.

![Triggers](/img/platform/workflows-triggers.png)

Search by trigger id, flow or namespace, and filter by **Namespace**, **Flow**, **Kind**, **Backfill** and **State**.

:::warning
When the worker is off the page shows *The worker is off, so triggered executions are created but do not run.* See [Flows](./flows) for how to enable it.
:::
