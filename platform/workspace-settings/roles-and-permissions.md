# Roles & permissions

**Workspace settings → Roles & permissions** shows what each workspace role can do.

![Workspace roles](/img/platform/settings-roles.png)

A fresh platform has three built-in roles:

| Role | Permissions |
|---|---|
| `ws_admin` | 25 |
| `ws_editor` | 17 |
| `ws_viewer` | 7 |

Expand a role to see its permissions. Use **Search roles or permissions…** to find a role by what it grants, and **Hide empty roles** to hide roles with no permissions.

:::note Read-only here
Roles are defined platform-wide in [Control Plane → Access control](../control-plane/access-control) and apply to every workspace. This page is a read-only view.
:::
