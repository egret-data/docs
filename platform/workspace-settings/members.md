# Members

**Workspace settings → Members** lists the people and groups that can use the workspace.

![Workspace members](/img/platform/settings-members.png)

The summary line counts members, admins, people and groups. For each member the table shows the **Type** (*User* or *Group*), the workspace **Role**, who added them and when. People who are platform administrators carry a *platform admin* tag.

- **Add member** adds a person or a group with a role.
- **Bulk select** lets you act on several members at once.
- The **⋯** menu on a row edits or removes that member.

Workspace roles apply only inside this workspace. See [Roles & permissions](./roles-and-permissions) for what each role grants. Platform-wide access is managed separately under [Control Plane → Access control](../control-plane/access-control).
