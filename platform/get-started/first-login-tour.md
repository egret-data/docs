# First login tour

After signing in you land in the **Explorer** for your default workspace.

![SQL Lab, the default landing page](/img/platform/explore-sql-lab.png)

## The top bar

- **Workspace picker** (top left): switch between the workspaces you belong to.
- **Explorer**: everyday work inside the current workspace.
- **Governance**: data access policy and classification.
- **Control Plane**: platform administration.
- **Theme toggle** and your **account menu** (top right).

## The Explorer sidebar

| Section | Pages |
|---|---|
| **Explore** | [SQL Lab](../explore/sql-lab), [Datasets](../explore/datasets-and-metrics), [Charts](../explore/charts), [Dashboards](../explore/dashboards), [Apps](../explore/apps), [Alerts & Reports](../explore/alerts-and-reports) |
| **Data API** | Endpoints, Clients, Activity |
| **Workflows** | Flows, Executions, Namespaces, Triggers |
| **Manage** | [Workspace settings](../workspace-settings/general) |

Use <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> for the command palette, and the **Collapse** button at the bottom to shrink the sidebar.

:::tip Where do I register a database?
Under **Manage → Workspace settings → Engines**, not under Explore. See [Databases](../workspace-settings/databases).
:::
