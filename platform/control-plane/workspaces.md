# Workspaces

**Control Plane → Workspaces** manages workspaces. Every workspace is an independent data platform with its own members and resources.

![Workspaces](/img/platform/control-plane-workspaces.png)

The header counts total, active and archived workspaces. The table shows each workspace's name and slug, **Owner**, **Created** date and **Status**; filter by status or search. **New workspace** creates one, and the **⋯** menu renames or archives it. Open a workspace to manage it.

Renaming or deleting happens here, not in the workspace's own [General](../workspace-settings/general) page.
